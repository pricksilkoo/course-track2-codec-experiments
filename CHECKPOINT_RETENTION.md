# Checkpoint retention audit — 2026-11-02

User authorized deletion of redundant historical weights while retaining the
best and other important weights. The audited pruning removed **301 files,
3.8444312187 GiB**. `/storage1` free space rose from approximately23GiB to27GiB.

The exact path, size and SHA-256 of each removed file are recorded in
`checkpoint_audits/20261102_applied.audit`. The dry run is preserved separately.
The script verified all **78 retained checkpoint-directory files** after pruning.
This count excludes the separately protected shared untrained `step0.pt` and
external checkpoints outside the inspected directories.

Retention deliberately treats each formal experiment's `best.pt` as an
important comparison artifact. This includes:

- frozen `strong_baseline_v2` and historical `strong_baseline_v1`;
- original official baseline, successful ancestor models and every formal
  study's selected positive/negative controls;
- the best competition-compliant development checkpoint, distillation peaks,
  uniform teacher and multi-seed comparators;
- research-only 40/60/80ms selected checkpoints, retained as clearly ineligible
  latency-study evidence;
- `raw_best.pt`, source steps20k/25k/30k of raw-distillation seeds3407/8128,
  and source steps2500/5000/7500/10000 of the two registered SWA runs;
- all checkpoints in unfinished runs, tracked weights and all symlinks.

Removed files were completed runs' redundant `last.pt` / numbered intermediate
snapshots and development smoke-test weights. Configurations, metrics, logs,
evaluation audio, fixed200 inputs, data and third-party weights were untouched.

These intermediate weights were deleted without a backup and **cannot be
undeleted through Git**. Their audit hashes identify them; exact artifacts can
only be regenerated by rerunning the recorded recipe, subject to numerical and
resume reproducibility limits. Final selected-weight evaluation remains
reproducible, while arbitrary historical continuation from removed steps is
intentionally no longer available. Future training retains best/last and two
recent snapshots to bound storage growth.

Script: `scripts/prune_completed_checkpoints.py` (dry-run by default).
